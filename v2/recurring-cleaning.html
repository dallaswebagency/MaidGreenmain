Recurring cleaning

Recurring house cleaning in Miami

Your home stays clean without the weekly scramble. The same trained team visits weekly, bi-weekly or monthly, with fully eco-friendly products and a plan tailored to your home.

Call 1.877.753.6243
What it is

Consistent clean, all year

In Miami, cleaning isn't seasonal, it's continuous. Humidity pushes dust through vents all year, pollen drifts in through open sliders, and salt air leaves a film on everything.

Recurring cleaning is a maintenance service carried out weekly, every two weeks or monthly, so grime, dust and clutter never build up between visits. It's the most popular choice with busy professionals and families. We serve Miami, Miami Beach, Coral Gables, Aventura and North Bay Village.

Every visit includes

A consistent checklist

Customized during your first appointment.

Kitchen

  • Sanitize countertops, stovetop and backsplash
  • Microwave interior and exterior
  • Scrub sink and polish faucet
  • Wipe appliance fronts
  • Take out trash, replace liner
  • Spot-clean cabinet fronts

Bathrooms

  • Scrub and disinfect toilet
  • Shower walls, tub and glass doors
  • Mirrors, vanity and countertop
  • Sink basin and faucet
  • Mop floors, wipe baseboards

Bedrooms

  • Make beds and straighten linens
  • Dust nightstands, dressers, shelving
  • Vacuum or dust-mop floors
  • Empty wastebaskets

Living areas

  • Dust surfaces, shelves and decor
  • Vacuum rugs and upholstered seating
  • Wipe tables and entertainment centers
  • Sliding-door glass (interior side)
  • Ceiling fan blades on rotation
Rotating deep tasks, free as standard: each visit includes one extra deep task, such as baseboards throughout, window sills and tracks, light switch and door handle sanitizing, or air vent and return grille dusting.
Frequency

Choose the frequency that fits your life

Weekly

Best for homes with children, pets and heavy daily use. Kitchens and bathrooms are cleaned before buildup occurs, at the lowest cost per visit.

Bi-weekly

The most popular option with our Miami clients. Keeps dust, grime and soap scum at bay, paired with light tidying in between.

Monthly

Suits smaller households and single-occupant condos that want a professional reset once a month.

Not sure? Start bi-weekly. You can always increase the frequency after a few visits.

FAQ

Recurring cleaning FAQ

How much does recurring cleaning cost?

It depends on home size, bathrooms and frequency. A biweekly plan for a typical 2-bedroom Miami home is usually $150 to $200 per visit; weekly plans have a lower per-visit rate.

What if I need to skip or reschedule?

Let us know at least 48 hours in advance and we'll move or cancel the visit. No penalty.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes: eco-friendly products, microfiber cloths, vacuum, mop and any specialist tools.

Will I have the same cleaners every time?

Yes, a fixed crew for every repeat client. If someone is out, we send a trained replacement and let you know in advance.

Can I customize what gets cleaned?

Definitely. Tell us what matters most and we'll tailor the checklist.

Should I start with a deep clean?

We recommend it. The deep clean is the reset; the recurring plan is the maintenance.

Are your products safe for babies and pets?

Yes. Plant-based, fragrance-free on request, and free of bleach, ammonia and volatile chemicals.
