Resources

Green cleaning tips from our pros.

Disinfect the bathroom

Mix 3% hydrogen peroxide and water 50/50 for bathrooms. Follow peroxide with vinegar to freshen cutting boards.

Hydrogen peroxideVinegar

Lift calcium buildup

A magic eraser and plain water removes buildup and stains from showers, tiles, walls and even shoes.

Magic eraserWater

Refresh your mugs

Soak mugs and coffee makers in a baking soda solution to remove stains and that bitter taste.

Baking soda

Remove sticky residue

Lemon oil lifts sticker and gum residue. A few drops in the washer keeps forgotten laundry from smelling.

Lemon essential oil

All-purpose spray

Vinegar, water and baking soda in a spray bottle handles sinks, toilets and showers.

White vinegarBaking soda
